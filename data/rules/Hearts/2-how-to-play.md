## How to Play

Your hand is shown face up when it is your turn. Instead of using the action buttons below the table you can click on your cards.

- **Passing.** At the start of a round, click a card to pass it. Pass three cards, one click each. The line above the action buttons says which way the cards go.
- **Playing.** Click a card to play it to the trick. The line above the action buttons shows the trick so far: who played what, and the suit led.

The cards you may pass or play have a yellow outline. A card without one cannot be played now, for example because you must follow the suit led.

**Tooltips.** Rest the mouse on a card to see what clicking it would do: who you would pass it to, or whether it follows suit, who would be winning the trick, how many points the trick would hold, and whether it breaks hearts. On a card you cannot play it says why.

A left click on any card in a hand still raises it to the front so you can see it whole, as before.
